Elemental Wizard

Elemental Wizard is a fast-paced 1v1 spell-shooter made in Godot, with 2D pixel-art graphics and an isometric view. Each player wields six spells, one for each element: water, fire, earth, air, electricity and nature. The combat system is inspired by real science: elements react with each other to apply debuffs, so combining air and fire, for example, triggers a Combustion debuff. Play against a friend locally or take on a CPU opponent with adjustable difficulty. Mix your spells, out-think your opponent, and be the last wizard standing.

Fruit Catcher

This Unity project is a 3D arcade-style game where the player controls a character to catch falling fruits and avoid bombs. The player moves using the mouse, and their size can be adjusted via the mouse scroll wheel. Each fruit caught increases the score by 1 point, while each bomb hit reduces the player's lives by 1. The player starts with 3 lives, visually represented by hearts. When all lives are lost, a game over screen appears, displaying the player's final score and offering the option to restart the game. The project emphasizes player movement, object interaction, and a simple scoring and lives system.

Car Shooter

Car Shooter is a thrilling 2D top-down arcade racer made in Unity that combines fast-paced racing with intense vehicular combat. Players control customisable cars, racing against AI opponents and using weapons like missiles and lasers to eliminate rivals. The game features various vibrant tracks, retro pixel-art graphics, and engaging gameplay mechanics, including drifting and braking, to enhance the racing experience.

Adventure Time

The objective is to guide the character through various obstacles, defeat enemies, collect valuable items, and reach the end goal to complete each level.

Obsidian Mirage

Obsidian Mirage is a unique and challenging 3D platformer game made in Unity as a group project. Players take on the role of Y2, a robot on a singular mission: to move forward and collect coins for their people. The game's objective is simple yet deceptively difficult: navigate through intricate and surreal landscapes filled with obsidian platforms, obstacles, and collectible coins.
